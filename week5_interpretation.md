- ## Interpretation

  This analysis compared treated versus control samples using DESeq2 with batch included in the design (`~ batch + condition`). The strongest QC observation was that treated and control samples separated clearly along PC1, which explained 24% of the variance, while batch contributed additional variation. After filtering, 989 genes were analyzed. Using padj < 0.05 and |shrunken log2 fold change| >= 1, 60 genes were significantly differentially expressed: 36 were upregulated and 24 were downregulated in treated samples. These results indicate that the treatment induced a broad transcriptional response rather than a change restricted to a small number of genes. However, biological pathways cannot be assigned from differential expression alone without gene annotation or pathway analysis. A limitation is the relatively small number of biological replicates within each batch. AI was used to guide the DESeq2 workflow and explain analysis steps; I independently verified sample matching, count validity, factor levels, model coefficients, DEG counts, and generated plots.
